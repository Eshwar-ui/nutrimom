Readability check (WCAG contrast)

Normal text needs a ratio of at least 4.5 and large text or icons at least 3.0. The brief's sage #A8BFA0 can't take white button text (about 2:1). That's why the proposal uses a deeper sage, #55704D, for buttons and keeps #A8BFA0 for backgrounds and accents.
